> **Repository edit:** kept as the run wrote it, except: the round-1 and round-2 visual reviews (7.1–7.3) are
> removed so only the final review remains, and the two `[review]` lines in the `score.py` output are shortened

Recreation of `assets/reference.mp4` as one paste-ready Shadertoy shader.
`work.glsl` is the source; `final.glsl` is `glsl_tools.py bundle work.glsl -o final.glsl`.
Every still and every video frame in `verify/` was rendered from `final.glsl` with
`tools/render.py` (headless Chromium, WebGL2 / ANGLE Metal) at 1280x720, iTime from the
frame's own pts, 24 fps.

## 2. Scene and camera

One fragment shader, two scene variants, a hard cut at `iTime = 3.625`.

- **Shot A (0-3.583 s, creek).** A heightfield creek (`baseA` + forest canopy) marched with
  the cabin SDF, a plank deck overhanging the water, two angled plank jetties with round
  bollards and two pale sagging ropes, boulders, explicit trees on the right bank, grass
  tufts, a crab, and the boat with the angler. The cabin has a gable roof with the ridge along
  x: long board courses, pale ridge/eave trim, a white-framed blue door and window, and a soft
  branch shadow. The deck is 8.6 x 9.2 world units (was 10 x 11.6 in round 2), with crates,
  a barrel, a fish trap, a buoy, a hose, a bucket, a plant, edge posts, a fascia and net rails.
  The camera moves upstream at 2.20 world units/s:
  `ro = (-0.76, 11.46, cz)`, `cz = 10.26 - 2.20*t`, `ta = (-0.33, 0.82, cz - 10.18)`,
  focal 2.31. The boat stays 8.76 units ahead of the camera, so the bank, cabin, deck and
  jetties scroll down the frame.
- **Shot B (3.625-14.125 s, bay).** A crater bay (`baseB`) with a rock ring, a carved
  waterfall inlet with a lip, stranded curtain and foam pool, boulders, forest on both flanks
  and flowers; the same boat with angler, long rod, line, bobber, ripple rings, fish and a
  wake. A pale landmark boulder (a rounded slab, not a sphere) sits left of the boat. The
  waterfall gets a soft mist glow at its base. The camera is a dolly-zoom from
  `ro = (0, 14.2, 10.8)`, `ta = (-0.2, 0, -1.9)`, focal 2.45 to `ro = (2.0, 10.0, 8.0)`,
  `ta = (1.4, 0, -2.2)`, focal 3.36. The push starts slowly with the shot, accelerates from
  6.8 to 7.4 s, then is static. The boat drifts from `(-0.9, 1.15)` to `(-2.55, -0.5)`.
- **Water.** An analytic plane at y = 0. Depth comes from the terrain height; the colour ramps
  from a sand/weed bed through shallow turquoise to mid/deep teal; a pale shallow shelf, low
  frequency caustic bands, two `waterWaveD` layers, Fresnel sky tint, sun glint, a thin broken
  shore foam band and wet line, a soft offset shadow under the bay boat, a soft cabin shadow
  on the creek and a tree shadow on the bay, a broken white wake/foam ring around the boat,
  fish silhouettes and a foam pool under the waterfall are added. The wide bay is graded
  duller than the close bay.
- **Lighting.** One warm wrapped sun with a heightfield shadow march, analytic soft blobs for
  the cabin, trees, boat and boulders, hemisphere ambient, a cool camera-side fill,
  `tonemapNeutral`, `linearToSrgb`, one dither tap. Large soft shadows are intended.

## 10. Notes on implementation

- `gShot` (0/1) selects the world; `mainImage` computes the camera, sets `gBoatPos`,
  `gBoatYaw`, `gBoatScale`, `gTime` and the shared uniforms before `map`/`shade`.
- The boat is authored in local units and scaled per shot: 1.15 in A, 1.22 -> 1.65 in B.
  The rod tip and line are transformed with the same yaw/scale.
- The cabin roof is a gable along x with two roof planes, long board courses, pale
  ridge/eave trim and a procedural branch shadow. The gable ends are at x = +-3.55, so the
  camera sees the long south slope rather than a triangular gable wall.
- The deck/piers are geometry, not textures: every prop is an SDF primitive with its own
  material id; the wood/rope/net colours are procedural. The pale bay boulder is a rounded
  box with `ID_ROCK_PALE`, so it keeps the rock shading but a lighter albedo.
- The water wake ring is a world-space band around the boat; an earlier build multiplied it
  by a falloff that was zero at the ring radius, so the ring never showed. The final build
  uses a falloff from 1.2 to 3.4 units and a noise break.
- The waterfall mist is a ray-to-base glow added after object shading, not a volumetric march;
  it gives a soft whitewater halo without a particle system.
- The score uses 320x180 nearest-neighbour classification; Q2 areas are sensitive to the
  class anchors at the sand/grass boundary, which is why the sand area is the only checkpoint
  over while all colour checks pass.
